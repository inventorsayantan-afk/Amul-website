How Amul Kool Rose is Made

water_drop

1. Sourcing Pure Milk

We begin by sourcing the highest quality, freshest milk from our extensive network of trusted dairy farmers across India. It undergoes rigorous testing for purity and nutrition.

local_florist

2. Extracting Natural Rose Essence

Real, fragrant rose petals are carefully selected to extract the authentic rose essence. This natural extract is what gives our drink its signature soothing aroma and flavor.

blender

3. The Perfect Blend

The pure milk and natural rose essence are expertly blended under strict hygienic conditions. We add just the right amount of sweetness to balance the flavor perfectly.

ac_unit

4. Sterilization & Chilling

The milk is subjected to Ultra High Temperature (UHT) treatment to ensure it is completely safe and free from preservatives, retaining its freshness. It's then rapidly chilled and packed.
